**Project:** CounterForge

**Short description:** A visual-model reliability laboratory that turns reviewed,
reproducible object-counting failures into portable regression tests.

**Problem and approach:** Image perturbations can expose a wrong answer while also
destroying the original ground truth. CounterForge retains reviewed source labels
and object masks, applies bounded mutations, independently checks their validity,
and requires fresh target confirmations before treating a failure as stable.
Evidence, budgets and review history stay attached to the result.

**Implemented Meta roles:** Spark planning and target evaluation, Muse Image
generation, SAM segmentation proposals. Human-reviewed masks and candidate
validity remain authoritative. Planner and target share a model family; no
independence or pinned binary-weight claim is made. See the existing provider
records for which roles have actual successful live evidence and their limits.
